# Question 1

## Why did Amazon create AWS?

### My Answer

Amazon realized that companies spent huge amounts of money buying servers, building data centers, maintaining infrastructure, and estimating future demand before launching their applications.

### Mentor's Feedback

Excellent understanding.

You correctly identified the business challenges. Remember that AWS allows companies to rent infrastructure instead of owning it.

### Final Understanding

AWS was created to solve the problems of expensive infrastructure, slow deployment, and difficult scaling by providing computing resources over the internet.

# Question 2

## What problems does cloud computing solve?

### My Answer

Cloud computing solves problems like buying hardware, building data centers, maintenance, electricity, cooling, security, scalability, and estimating future demand.

### Mentor's Feedback

Very good answer.

To make it even stronger, explain not only the problems but also how cloud computing solves each one.

### Final Understanding

Cloud computing eliminates the need to own infrastructure by providing on-demand computing resources that are scalable, reliable, and cost-effective.

# Question 3

## What is a data center?

### My Answer

A data center is a place containing many servers that provide IT resources for businesses.

### Mentor's Feedback

Good answer.

Remember that a data center is much more than servers. It also includes networking, cooling, backup power, physical security, and fire protection.

### Final Understanding

# Question 4

### My Answer

A restaurant prepares only the food you order. You don't build the kitchen or hire the chef—you simply request the meal and pay for it.

### Mentor's Feedback

Excellent analogy.

### Final Understanding

# Question 5

## What is Pay-as-you-go?

### My Answer

You pay only while using the service and avoid paying for resources that are sitting idle.

### Mentor's Feedback

Correct.

Think of it like paying an electricity bill—you are charged based on actual usage.

### Final Understanding

Pay-as-you-go is a pricing model where customers pay only for the cloud resources they actually consume.
