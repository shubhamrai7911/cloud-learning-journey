
## Mistake 1

### I Thought

A data center was simply a room full of servers.

### Mentor's Feedback

A data center also includes networking, cooling, backup power, physical security, and fire protection.

### Final Understanding

A data center is a complete environment designed to keep IT systems running reliably.

## Mistake 2

### I Thought

Pay-as-you-go only meant paying monthly.

### Mentor's Feedback

It means paying based on actual resource usage, regardless of the billing period.

### Final Understanding

Cloud pricing is based on consumption, not ownership.
