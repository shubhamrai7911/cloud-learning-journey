# Cloud Computing Fundamentals

## Why AWS Was Created

Before cloud computing, companies had to:

- Buy expensive servers
- Build and maintain data centers
- Predict future demand
- Spend large amounts of money before launching products

AWS solved these problems by allowing businesses to rent computing resources over the internet.

---

## Cloud Computing

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services over the internet.

Instead of owning infrastructure, users can access resources whenever they need them.

---

## Data Center

A data center is a specially designed facility that houses servers and supporting infrastructure.

A data center includes:

- Servers
- Networking equipment
- Cooling systems
- Backup power (UPS & generators)
- Physical security
- Fire protection

---

## On-Demand

Resources are available whenever needed.

Users can start or stop services without purchasing hardware.

---

## Pay-as-you-go

Customers pay only for the resources they use.

No upfront investment is required.

---

## Scalability

Cloud resources can increase or decrease based on demand.

This allows businesses to handle changing workloads efficiently.
